4. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores learning-related information locally, including:

  • words and lessons you have viewed;
  • learning, repetition, and completion status;
  • completed quizzes;
  • bookmarked words;
  • the current lesson and study position;
  • App language, theme, and display preferences;
  • pronunciation and autoplay preferences;
  • flashcard settings; and
  • word-widget information.

The developer does not intentionally transmit your individual learning progress, bookmarks, or quiz results to developer-controlled servers.

Local information may leave the device only when:

  • Android includes it in an operating-system backup or device transfer;
  • you create a backup and save it to local or cloud storage;
  • you share an exported backup file; or
  • a device service selected by you processes the information.

5. Backup and Restore Files

The App allows you to export learning progress and preferences as a JSON backup file. Android lets you choose where that file is stored. The selected location may be local device storage or a third-party provider such as Google Drive. The developer does not receive or control the exported file.

Backup files may include:

  • learning and repetition progress;
  • bookmarks and quiz status;
  • language and theme settings;
  • display preferences; and
  • pronunciation preferences.

Backup files are not separately encrypted by the App. Anyone who obtains a backup file may be able to read it. You are responsible for protecting, sharing, retaining, and deleting your backup files.

When you restore a backup, the App reads the file you select and stores its supported contents locally. The file is not intentionally uploaded to the developer. A cloud-storage provider processes files under its own privacy policy and terms.

6. Android System Backup and Device Transfer

The Android App currently permits operating-system backup. Depending on your Android version, device manufacturer, Google Account, and backup settings, Android may back up some App data to private cloud backup storage, restore it after reinstallation, or transfer it to another device.

These operations are controlled by Android, Google, the device manufacturer, or the selected backup provider—not by the developer. Uninstalling the App may not immediately delete copies retained by an operating-system backup.

For more information, review Android's Auto Backup documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Text-to-Speech and Pronunciation

Most pronunciation audio is provided from resources included with the App. If local audio is unavailable, the App may send the selected German word or example sentence to the text-to-speech engine installed or selected on your device.

A text-to-speech engine may operate locally or use an online service. Whether text is transmitted outside the device depends on the engine, voice, provider, and device settings. The provider's privacy policy applies to its processing. The App does not request microphone access and does not record your voice.
